Your notes are yours.
We act like it.

Every mainstream notes app quietly makes a photocopy of your brain and keeps it in someone else's filing cabinet. LostNote doesn't have a cabinet. Your notes live in your device's own encrypted database — and nowhere else.

Dress them in glass-tint colors, staple photos to them, export the whole stack to PDF when paper wins. Lock the app with a PIN, and hide the sensitive ones in an archive behind a second PIN.

Deleted things wait politely in trash with one-tap undo. Nothing vanishes by accident.
