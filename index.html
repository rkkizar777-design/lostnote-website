Not another notes app.
Your second memory.

Most notes apps hold your thoughts hostage in someone else's cloud. LostNote doesn't have a cloud. Your notes live in your device's own database, encrypted by your OS, readable only by you.

Dress them up with glass-tint colors, attach photos, tag them, pin them. Export everything to PDF when paper wins. Lock the sensitive stuff behind a PIN — and hide it completely in an archive with its second PIN.

The killer move: open LostNote on your phone and PC, type one 5-digit code, and your entire library crosses over — including archived and trashed notes, exactly as they are. No cable. No account. No cloud.
